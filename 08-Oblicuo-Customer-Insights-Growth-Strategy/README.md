## The Challenge

Oblicuo is a Barcelona listening bar inspired by Japanese Hi-Fi culture. The business question was:

> **How could the venue increase Tuesday–Thursday visits without losing its premium, intimate and locally relevant identity?**

The challenge was not maximum reach. Broad visibility could attract poorly matched audiences and weaken the experience that differentiated the venue.

## What We Did

- competitor and platform analysis;
- a **five-persona framework**;
- customer journey analysis;

## Key Insights

1. Social media often validated a venue choice rather than created initial discovery.
2. The main barrier was uncertainty: crowd level, noise, seating and atmosphere.
3. Different audiences valued the Hi-Fi concept for different reasons.
4. Weekday demand required predictability rather than hype.

## Priority Segment

The most relevant weekday segment was the **After-Work Neighbor**: a nearby professional looking for one good drink and a comfortable conversation without planning a full night out.

The team developed four customer personas and one collaborator persona.

<p align="center">
  <img src="./assets/07_persona_map.png" alt="Oblicuo persona framework" width="70%">
</p>

## Recommendation

The strategy repositioned social media as a **risk-reduction tool**.

Content should show:

- early-evening atmosphere;
- available seating and conversation-friendly conditions;
- proximity and convenience;
- familiar staff, drinks and a repeatable weekday ritual.

The objective was to convert local consideration into spontaneous Tuesday–Thursday visits.
